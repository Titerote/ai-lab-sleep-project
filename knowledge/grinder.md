# Grinder

## Settings

- Espresso: 14 on the dial for the house blend; 12 for light roasts.
- Dose 18 g in, 36 g out, 27–30 s.

## Cleaning

Run 20 g of grinder-cleaning pellets monthly, then purge 10 g of beans.
Brush the burrs every 2 kg of coffee.

## Burr life

Steel burrs are rated for roughly 500 kg; the current set was installed
2025-03 and has seen about 40 kg.
