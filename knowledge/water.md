# Water

## Filter

The in-tank filter cartridge (BWT-style) is changed every 60 days. Write the
date on the cartridge sleeve.

## Effect on descaling

Descale every 150 shots whether or not the filter is installed; see
descaling.md. (Changed 2026-11-12 from 300 shots with the filter, on the
user's instruction.)

## Hardness

Tap water here measures 9 °dH (test strip, 2026-09-14). Anything above 7 °dH
counts as hard for this machine.
